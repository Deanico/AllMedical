Patient Portal Privacy Notice

Effective date: August 25, 2026 | Version: 2026-08-25

This Privacy Notice explains how All Medical, LLC, doing business as AllMedical, handles information in connection with the Patient Portal (the "Portal"). It supplements, and does not replace, the AllMedical Notice of Privacy Practices, which describes uses and disclosures of protected health information when HIPAA applies.

Information We Collect and Display

We collect or display information needed to provide and secure Portal access, including your name, email address, account credentials, account identifiers, contact information, product and order information, shipment information, insurance-related account information, and technical information generated when you use the Portal, such as device, browser, log, and security-event information.

How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users; provide Portal features; coordinate products, orders, deliveries, and customer support; maintain records; protect against unauthorized access, fraud, and misuse; comply with legal obligations; and improve Portal reliability and security. We use and disclose protected health information only as permitted or required by applicable law and our Notice of Privacy Practices.

How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers and business associates that help us operate the Portal, deliver products and services, provide hosting, security, communications, payment, fulfillment, or support. We may also share information when required by law, to protect rights, safety, and security, or as otherwise permitted by applicable law. We require service providers handling protected health information to protect it as required by law and contract.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, keep it confidential, and sign out when using a shared or public device. Contact AllMedical promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain legally required records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet our legal, regulatory, accounting, and operational obligations.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may have rights to access, inspect, receive a copy of, request correction of, request restrictions on, or receive an accounting of disclosures of your protected health information. Your applicable Notice of Privacy Practices explains these rights and how to exercise them.

Children's Information

The Portal is not intended for independent use by children. A parent, guardian, or other authorized representative may use the Portal on behalf of a patient where permitted by law and authorized by AllMedical.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice to reflect changes in our practices, Portal features, or legal requirements. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes will be posted through the Portal or another appropriate communication channel.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Notice, to request the applicable Notice of Privacy Practices, or to exercise privacy rights, contact All Medical, LLC at 352-328-8308, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Time.
